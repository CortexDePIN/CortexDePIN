The bill

Renting a GPU still does not prove the work.

The buyer needs capacity, a price, and a reason to believe this input produced this output. Cloud sells the machine. The proof is still a log from the party that got paid.

What breaks

Three ways the payment comes loose from the job.

  1. 01
    The hour is the wrong unit.

    Reserved capacity bills the window, including minutes the model was not running. A short inference job still pays for the reservation around it.

  2. 02
    A listing is not a result.

    Spot GPUs are easy to post. Nothing in the payment checks the model, the input hash, or the output hash against the card that was advertised.

  3. 03
    Online is not the same as useful.

    A network that rewards uptime pays for a heartbeat. The requester paid for tokens, or for a training step that moved the loss.

Thesis

Settle the slice. Return the rest.

One signature locks escrow. Each attested slice releases a micropayment. Unused buffer goes back to the requester when the job closes.

The loop

Three moves. One signature from the requester.

The proof

No signature, no release.

The payment rule is the product. A slice leaves escrow only after the input, the output, and the advertised GPU are committed together.

In this demo the quorum is simulated. The rule is not: a failed check does not pay.

Same job, two seats

The requester funds it. The provider earns it.

Requester

Lock, then read.

They pick a GPU, a model, and a prompt or a training note. After the signature they do not manage the machine.

  • Balance drops when escrow locks, not when the hour ends.
  • The buffer returns if the window is not used.

Provider

Post, then get paid.

They list hardware at an hourly rate. Earnings show up only when a job on that node is attested and the escrow settles.

  • A busy node is not bookable.
  • A heartbeat alone does not settle.

Why this network

A slice has to be cheap enough to be a payment.

The job is a burst, not a monthly invoice. Settlement has to finish inside it, and the requester should not sign again to get the refund.
